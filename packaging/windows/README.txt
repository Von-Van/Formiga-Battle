Formiga Battle

Once Formiga Battle is installed with its installer, Formiga Desktop will find it on its own and
open it with your companions. (This portable copy runs without installing, but Desktop does not
look for it, so the installer is the easier way to go.)

The download is not signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask before it runs the first time.

Formiga Battle never changes your colony. Desktop keeps everything a match earns.
